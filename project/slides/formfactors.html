The phone goes into StandBy · not designed for

iPhone Duo can be held six ways. Five are in scope: closed; open in portrait and in landscape; and half folded in portrait and in landscape. The half-folded states reuse the open layouts. The sixth, the cover screen with the phone semi-folded, puts the phone into StandBy, so we are not designing noon for it.
