The same five tabs, in the same order

On iPhone, noon's five tabs float along the bottom. On the Duo's cover screen they stand up as a vertical rail in the side column, under the camera and status bar, so content keeps the full height. On the open screen the rail stays on the trailing edge. In split view it moves to noon's outer edge; see the split view principle.
